# Privacy Policy — Composio Personal Assistant

**Last updated:** September 8, 2026
**Owner:** Juan David Baquero Bermudez
**Contact:** juanda217bb@gmail.com

## 1. Scope

This policy covers the personal, non-commercial integration "Composio Personal Assistant",
which connects my own TikTok account to my personal AI assistant (via Composio).
Single user: only me. There are no other users.

## 2. Data I collect

Only data from my own TikTok account that I explicitly authorize through TikTok's
OAuth consent screen, limited to the granted scopes:

- Basic profile info (e.g., display name, avatar, open ID).
- Account statistics I request (e.g., follower/video counts).
- My own video list metadata I request.

I do not collect data from other TikTok users.

## 3. How I use data

Data is used solely to answer my own requests inside my personal assistant
(e.g., showing my profile info or summarizing my own content analytics).

## 4. Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share TikTok data with third parties. The only processors
involved are the infrastructure that runs the assistant:

- Composio (integration broker between TikTok and the assistant).

No advertising, no profiling of third parties, no cross-app tracking.

## 5. Storage and retention

Data is processed transiently to answer each request. I keep no separate database
of TikTok data. OAuth tokens are stored securely by the integration broker and can
be revoked at any time.

## 6. Your rights / revocation

As the sole user, I can revoke access at any time from my TikTok account settings
(Authorized apps) or by deleting the app connection, which stops all future data
access. To request deletion of any residual data, contact juanda217bb@gmail.com.

## 7. Contact

Questions about this policy: juanda217bb@gmail.com.
